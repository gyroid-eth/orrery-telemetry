Portraits used by the demo
==========================

One 64px portrait per agent, across every story, copied from
dashboard/portraits_64/. build.sh ships only the surnames the cast uses.

Provenance: the portraits are pixel art generated by the author (gyroid)
with ChatGPT image generation from text prompts only; no photographs were
used as input. dashboard/portraits_64/ holds 64px reductions of the
originals in dashboard/portraits_pixel/. They are distributed under the
same terms as the repository (PolyForm Perimeter License 1.0.1).

See docs/third-party.md in the repository for the full record.
