Portraits used by the demo
==========================

Nine 64px portraits ship with this bundle, one per agent in the fixture.
All are from Wikimedia Commons and all are in the public domain — checked
against the Commons API, not assumed. A tenth (Rosalind Franklin, MRC LMB)
was dropped rather than shipped: it is CC BY-SA 4.0, and a cropped 64px
derivative would carry the ShareAlike obligation into a product demo.
