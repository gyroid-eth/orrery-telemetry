Portraits used by the demo
==========================

One 64px portrait per agent, across every story. All are from Wikimedia
Commons and all are in the public domain — checked against the Commons API,
not assumed. build.sh refuses to ship a surname that is not listed in
PORTRAITS_CLEARED.txt, so the licence check is a build step rather than
something someone has to remember.

Rosalind Franklin (MRC LMB) was dropped: CC BY-SA 4.0, and a cropped 64px
derivative would carry the ShareAlike obligation into a product demo.
